What is the Bet of the Day?
Bet of the Day is our best betting choice for a given day. It's picked by our team of experts out of all matches that the current football fixture list has to offer.
It is a carefully realised betting tip that brings high added value. Each day we create two daily bets, a double and a risk, with a slightly higher odd.
